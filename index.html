How our monthly accessibility check-up works

Every active site gets one accessibility scan a month. We fix what it finds, confirm the fixes, and report back to the client. This page walks through it start to finish.

Page budget

Staying inside 250 pages a month

Our Marker plan covers 250 scanned pages a month across every site. That's why scans are scheduled by hand instead of running automatically. With 5 active sites, a month looks like this:

100
40
50
60
0250 pages
100Full scans: 5 sites × 20 pages
~40Rescans: 5 sites × about 8 pages
~50Buffer for surprises like theme updates or launches
~60Spare

When we grow to 9 sites, a month would need about 252 pages, just over the limit. At that point we'll use lighter scans for sites that share a theme, or move quieter sites to every other month. Each ticket records the pages it used, and a Notion view adds them up by month, so we check it before booking the next scan.

The store safety check

Most accessibility fixes aren't visible, so what clients care about most is that nothing broke. After every update we check these and list them as passed:

  • Main menu and mobile menu
  • Product page: picking options and adding to cart
  • Cart: changing quantity and removing items
  • Checkout starts from the cart
  • Search returns results
  • Contact and newsletter forms send
  • Login and register
Behind the scenes

How results reach Notion

Marker doesn't send a signal when a scan finishes, so our developer starts this hand-off with one command once the scan is done. Everything after that is automatic.

1 · MarkerScan finishesStarted by hand in Marker.
2 · Claude CodeOne commandReads the failing checks for that site.
3 · MakeSorts themFinds the site, finds or creates this month's ticket, skips anything already added.
4 · NotionTicket and to-dosReady for the team to write the first report.

If a ticket doesn't show up

Make handles one job at a timeA failed job waits in Make's "Incomplete executions" list, and new ones queue behind it until it's cleared.
Notion must stay connected to MakeThe Sites, Accessibility Scan and Accessibility Issues databases all need the Make connection.
The report template is added by handThe ticket arrives without its page layout, so we apply the template at step 4.

Adding a new site

  1. Add the site to the Sites database in Notion with its name, project, web address and Marker project ID. Without this, no ticket is created.
  2. If it belongs to a new project, add that project as an option on Accessibility Scan.
  3. Turn off automatic scans for the site in Marker, and create its calendar reminders.
